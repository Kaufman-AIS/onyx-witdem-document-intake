# Enterprise Refund Policy

Enterprise customers on annual contracts may request a full refund within **30 days**
of the initial purchase date. Refunds are processed to the original payment method
within 5–10 business days after approval.

To start a refund request, contact **support@example.com** with your account ID and
invoice number. Partial refunds are not available after the 30-day window.

Self-serve customers on monthly plans can cancel anytime; unused time is not refunded.
